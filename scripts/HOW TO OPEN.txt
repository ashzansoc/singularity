Singularity — how to open on macOS
====================================

If macOS says the app is "damaged", that is Gatekeeper (not a bad file).

1. Open the DMG and drag Singularity.app into Applications.
2. Paste these three lines into Terminal (no extra comments):

xattr -cr /Applications/Singularity.app
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Singularity.app
open /Applications/Singularity.app

Do not open Singularity from inside the disk image.
